VMK - what's new
================

v1.2.0 (2026-10-24) - Haunted Mansion rooms, Blue Bayou, server fixes
---------------------------------------------------------------------

  - The Haunted Mansion is open all the way through: the Stretching
    Room, the Ballroom and the Attic, reached from the crypt in the
    Haunted Mansion Lobby, and on to the Frontierland Monorail.
  - The hitchhiking ghosts are back. Phineas haunts the Stretching
    Room, Ezra the Ballroom and Gus the Attic. They come and go, drift
    about the room and talk; you may have to wait for one to appear.
    Double-click a ghost when you find it for credits (40 the first
    time, 20 every 12 hours after).
  - The three mansion rooms are furnished: cupboards, busts, clocks,
    candles, handrails, the Ballroom's tables and presents, the Attic's
    harp and chests, and the doors.
  - The Blue Bayou has its restaurant tables and chairs, and the Gator
    Waiter. Take a seat, wave him down and say the magic word, and he
    brings your food to the table. Double-click him for credits like
    the other characters.
  - Server fixes for anyone hosting on the internet: a single malformed
    web request (a broken % escape in the address or in a cookie, or an
    odd Host header) could stop the server. Oversized game messages are
    refused, connections that never log in are closed after a minute,
    and the client error log can no longer grow without limit.
    Logged-in players who stand idle stay connected (2 hours by
    default; set VMK_IDLE_TIMEOUT_MINUTES to change it, 0 for no limit).
  - Security: the GetSec addresses (sec.php, loading.php), which only
    the original Shockwave client uses, handed out a signed-in player's
    login ticket to anyone who asked, the owner's included. They are now
    off unless VMK_SHOCKWAVE_GETSEC=1 is set, and even then only give a
    player their own. Login tickets work once, and are no longer written
    to the logs. If you host on the internet, update.

v1.1.4 (2026-10-21) - Turnstiles, room smoke and fire
-----------------------------------------------------

  - Turnstiles turn properly whichever way they face. Facing two of the
    four directions, a turned turnstile (green, purple, red, blue and
    gold) was drawn mirrored, with its door hanging loose in front of the
    post. The scoreboard fix in v1.1.3 caused this; scoreboards still
    light up on the right side.
  - The campfire in the tent sits on its logs again instead of floating
    above them, and the tent's smoke is back.
  - Missing animations restored: the water and smoke in the cannon room,
    the robot in Galaxy Imports and the smoke from Ned's cauldron.
  - Smoke is see-through and fades as it rises, instead of being drawn
    solid, and the cannon room smoke no longer blinks.
  - Editing a room description: the typing cursor follows the text onto
    the next line, instead of staying at the end of the first line.

v1.1.3 (2026-10-19) - One-way teleporters, scoreboard lights
------------------------------------------------------------

  - Scoreboards light up on the right side when turned. Facing one way,
    the lit bars of the gold, purple, red, blue and green scoreboards were
    drawn on the wrong face of the board; they now follow the board's
    direction. The same fix applies to a few other animated items that
    reuse one side's art for the other.
  - Ride teleporters only work one way round, as in the original. A ride
    enters a teleporter only through its open end (the side it faces, where
    its track joins) and comes out of the partner's open end. Riding into
    the back or side of a teleporter now ends the ride in front of it.
    If a ride in your room stopped teleporting after this update, turn the
    entry teleporter so its open end faces back along the track.

v1.1.2 (2026-10-19) - Getting off rides
---------------------------------------

  - At the end of a ride you now get off onto the next tile, the way you
    were riding, instead of being left standing on the last piece of
    track. If that tile is taken (furniture, more track, someone standing
    there, or the edge of the room) you stay on the last piece and can
    walk off to any free tile beside it.
  - Same when a ride comes out of a teleporter with no track beyond it:
    you get off on the far side of the teleporter.

  Tip for ride teleporters: the open end of the dark-ride teleporters
  (Alice, Peter Pan, Royal) faces one way. At the end of a track, turn it
  so the open end faces back along the track; the partner faces the way
  you leave. Rides work either way round; this is only how it looks.

v1.1.1 (2026-10-19) - Teleporter fixes
--------------------------------------

  - Teleporters bought in the shop now come as a linked pair. The Space
    Mountain Teleporter used to arrive as one lone half, which could never
    send anyone anywhere. Staff "give" in /hk hands out pairs too.
    A lone half bought before this update still does nothing; staff can
    give the player a new pair.
  - The Magic Show Water Trap Teleporter and the Tower of Terror Elevator
    Teleporter now work. They had no teleport pad, so stepping on them did
    nothing.
  - Ride teleporters (River, Yeti, Space Mountain, Waterslide, Alice and
    Peter Pan) now carry the ride on. A ride that reaches a teleporter
    whose partner is placed comes out of the partner, in the same room or
    another guest room, and keeps riding along the track there. Before, the
    ride just stopped on the teleporter.

v1.1.0 (2026-10-19) - Faster loading
------------------------------------

Loading was slow when VMK runs on a server: every file costs a trip over
the internet, and the game made thousands of them. Measured with 100 ms
of network delay per request (a typical internet connection):

  First visit, start-up to the park map
    Before:  1,826 downloads, 56 MB, still downloading after a minute
    Now:       204 downloads, 17 MB, map in about 17 seconds

  Coming back (reload / next visit)
    Game code and unchanged art are kept by the browser: a return visit to
    Main Street downloads about 1 MB instead of re-downloading everything.

  Entering a room (Main Street -> Town Square)
    About 3 MB and 6 seconds, down from roughly twice that.

  Release size
    5.2 GB -> 2.0 GB. Every picture is stored losslessly compressed: the
    pixels are identical, the files are about a quarter of the size.

What changed:
  - The server compresses text and game code (gzip/brotli) and lets
    browsers keep files they already have; a changed file is always
    fetched fresh.
  - Pictures are fetched many at a time in one download instead of one by
    one.
  - The next areas in the loading queue start downloading while the current
    one is still being set up (the order the game sets them up in is
    unchanged).
  - Less work for the browser after logging in: the pause before the map
    appears is roughly halved.

Also fixed:
  - Fantasyland Courtyard: walking onto the exit spots by the Alice and
    Peter Pan rides put you into the ride's room for free, without riding.
    That was never intended, so those exits are now disabled. Boarding
    through the turnstiles is unchanged.
  - Christmas snow drew as a strip of white boxes along the top of every
    Christmas room. It now falls as white flakes, as in the original.

Seasons: the Christmas decorations are OFF by default. To turn a season on,
set VMK_SEASON=xmas (or halloween) for both servers; see SELF-HOSTING.md.

For hosts: nothing to configure. A reverse proxy (e.g. Caddy) in front of
VMK needs no compression setting of its own - VMK compresses its own
responses.
