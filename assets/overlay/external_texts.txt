pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.1=CONTROLS \r Left Mouse Button Single Click - Your ship will sail towards where you click \r Left Mouse Button Double Click - Fires cannons on the side of the ship that you click \r Left & Right Arrow Key - Double press an arrow key to fire to that side of your ship. (A single press shows a preview of where your cannons will fire.)
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.2=THE GOAL \r Two teams of up to four players each battle it out at sea. You'll captain your very own Pirate ship, unloading your cannons at your enemies and helping your team to victory. Fire at your enemies until their ships are sunk. There are three rounds, each one ending when one side has lost all of its ships.
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.3=HOW TO START \r The POTC Game Lobby is where the battles begin. You can join a game that someone else is starting, or start a new game of your own. The games, teams, and team members are shown in the lower part of the screen. Use the arrow buttons on the sides of the games list to browse through the games that are about to start. You can play Level 2 and Level 3 games after you've won enough games at the previous level.
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.4=HOW TO NAVIGATE \r When the game starts, your ship is indicated by an arrow. Your ship will be Blue or Red, and all the ships on your side will be the same color. Click the map on the spot you want to get to and your ship will start sailing towards it. A blue wheel marks the spot you clicked on. The ship won't navigate for you, so make sure there's open water between you and your destination.
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.5=HOW TO SHOOT \r Double press the arrow keys - or double-click with your mouse on the map - to shoot. The ship will fire a volley of cannonballs from the side you choose. Your ship has limited ammo so make sure you take aim before you fire! Press the arrow key once to check where your cannons are aimed. When ships are hit, they get damaged and the captain loses health. Your health is shown as first-aid crates at the bottom of the screen.
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.6=STAYING IN THE GAME \r You can pick up more ammo and health by sailing over the floating cannonballs and crates in the sea. But be quick - everyone else is after them, too. After you've taken your final hit, your burned-out ship remains floating on the sea. You can explode your gunpowder reserves by double-clicking your shipwreck. Time your explosion carefully and you might take your enemies down with you!
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.7=LEVEL 2 \r You'll discover new powers in the second level, where you can transform into an invincible ghost ship by sailing into the moonlight. But it's not all good. You have to keep well clear of the dizzying whirlpool, and make sure the ferocious sea monster doesn't catch you. As soon as you've won 10 games at Level 2, find Captain Blackheart in the Pirate Treehouse to gain access to Level 3.
pirates.classic.instructions.text.page.8=LEVEL 3 \r Once you've won enough second-level games, and visited Captain Blackheart in the Pirate Treehouse, you can progress to the third and final level. Shoot the cannon towers for extra points. But watch out... they can shoot back and spill flaming oil! Pressing the ghost button turns your ship to the invincible ghost ship for a moment.
